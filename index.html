Your Windows PC,
on your Chromebook.

RemoteLink streams your PC's screen and sound to any Chromebook browser and sends your mouse and keyboard back, with low latency, from anywhere. Nothing gets installed on the Chromebook.

Press Enter to open the app · D to download the PC app

How it works

A tiny app on the PC waits quietly in the tray. You start every connection from the Chromebook.

🤝

Handshake

The Chromebook and your PC find each other through a private, encrypted room. This takes a few seconds and carries no screen data.

⚡

Direct stream

Video goes straight between the two computers with WebRTC, encoded by your graphics card at up to 60 fps. On strict school networks, an optional Cloudflare relay takes over.

🖱️

Real control

Mouse, keyboard, scroll, clipboard and special keys. Desktop mode keeps text sharp; Game mode captures the mouse for smooth camera movement.

Set it up in 3 steps

About 10 minutes, once.

01 · ON YOUR PC

Install the PC app

  1. Download RemoteLink.zip and extract it somewhere permanent.
  2. Open host and double-click install.bat.
  3. Choose a password and scan the QR code with an authenticator app on your phone.
02 · ON THE CHROMEBOOK

Pair once

  1. Open the RemoteLink app and bookmark it.
  2. Type the pairing code your PC shows (tray icon → Settings & pairing).
03 · CONNECT

Log in

  1. First time: password + the 6-digit code, with “Remember this Chromebook” ticked.
  2. After that: just your password and Enter.
On the PC, set Windows to never ask to sign in again after being away (Settings → Accounts → Sign-in options), and don't lock it with Win+L before you leave. Windows doesn't allow the lock screen to be controlled remotely.

Built to be safe

Your PC is never exposed to the internet: no open ports, and nobody gets in without your password.

  • Your password never travels, only a one-time proof of it.
  • Authenticator code on the first login from every new device.
  • Trusted Chromebooks can be removed at any time from the PC.
  • Everything is encrypted: the handshake with AES-256, the stream by WebRTC.
  • Lockout after 5 wrong tries (15 minutes, then longer).
  • Panic key on the PC: Ctrl+Alt+Shift+K ends the session and blocks new logins.
  • View-only mode, enforced by the PC itself.
  • Alerts on every login, failed attempt and lockout, plus an activity log.
School Chromebooks are usually monitored, so whatever you open on your PC can be visible to the school. Use a strong password, and check your school's rules about remote access.

Help

“Your PC didn't answer”

The PC must be on, awake and online, with the RemoteLink tray icon cyan (it may be hidden under the ^ arrow next to the clock).

“The stream didn't start”

The network blocks direct video connections. Add the free Cloudflare relay in the PC settings (tray → Settings & pairing → Settings).

Black screen

The PC is on the lock screen or showing an administrator (UAC) prompt. Windows doesn't allow either to be controlled remotely.

It feels laggy

Open stats with Ctrl+Alt+Shift+S. “Relay” adds a little delay; lower the bandwidth to Balanced or use 30 fps on weak Wi-Fi. A network cable on the PC helps.

Keyboard shortcuts in the app

Hold Ctrl+Alt+Shift and press F full screen · G game mode · S stats · H toolbar · T type text · M sound · C second cursor · Q disconnect. The Search key works as the Windows key.
